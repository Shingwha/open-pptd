# Poster / Infographic — Single Page Only

Use this file only for a poster, infographic, or highly visual single page. It
is not a template for ordinary presentations.

**Audience:** conference, exhibition, and open-day visitors reading from 1–2 m
for 20–90 seconds. **Reader task:** get one claim, one piece of evidence, and
one reason to talk to the author, then find the paper or contact.

## Reading order

```text
title/authors → one-sentence claim → abstract → method and data →
results (main evidence) → conclusion → references + QR
```

Visitors read in a Z pattern: title, top-left block, main figure, bottom band.
Put the claim where the eye lands first; never lead with institutional logos.

## Canvas and safe area

- A-series portrait ratio is 1:√2. In PPTD write `size: [1191, 1684]` for A2 at
  72dpi, or `[1684, 1191]` for landscape. A0/A1 use the same ratio at 2×/1.41×.
- Never use `[960, 540]` or stretch a 16:9 slide into a poster. If the venue
  supplies a size or template, rebuild the zones to that specification.
- Safe margin is at least 6% of the short side (72px on A2) on every edge.
  Only background reaches trim; title, QR, and conclusion stay inside it.
- Column count follows content: one for statement-led, two for method/result
  pairs, three only for a wide results wall. Gutter ≥32px.

## Content contracts

- **Title:** claim or precise question, two lines maximum; authors, presenting
  author, affiliation/contact, and accurate venue/poster number. Logos are one
  small strip and never larger than authors.
- **Abstract:** 100–180 words in three to five sentences: problem, approach with
  sample/scale, claim with key number, optional implication. No citation wall or
  copied paper abstract; numbers match results exactly.
- **Method/data:** state n, unit of analysis, period, setting, real instrument /
  version, procedure, comparison, and analysis. Keep at most three blocks and six
  short lines; detailed protocol belongs behind the QR link.
- **Results:** one main complete figure carries the result. Repeat two or three
  decisive numbers with unit and n in a rail, plus a short reading. A compact
  table is allowed only when categorical comparison is clearer than a figure.
- **Conclusion:** at most three bullets answering the title, effect size and
  condition, plus one quotable takeaway. Observational `associated with` does not
  become causal `reduced`.
- **References/QR:** 5–8 real references in one style, with DOI/stable URL where
  useful. QR is at least 140px at A2 (200px preferred), labelled with what it
  opens, and accompanied by a short text link when possible.
- Use three to five evidence blocks on the whole poster. Cut content rather than
  shrinking below the print sizes below.

## Layout grammars

Choose one continuous reading path, not a collage of unrelated zones.

- **Title:** full-width top band; title-left/key-figure-right; or one short
  statement with authors below. Keep logos smaller than authors.
- **Abstract:** 100–180 word full-width block; problem/approach versus
  claim/implication columns on a landscape board; or 40–48px claim above
  26–28px body copy. Never trap the abstract in a narrow side column.
- **Method / data:** three compact sample/procedure/analysis blocks; design
  diagram left with notes right; or horizontal longitudinal timeline. Keep it
  smaller than results.
- **Results:** complete main figure occupies 40–55% of the content area, with a
  narrow 2–3 number rail or 60%/40% figure/reading split.
- **Conclusion:** one full-width takeaway band with at most three bullets; a
  found/means two-column block; or one 44–54px statement plus qualification.
- **References / QR:** references bottom-left with QR bottom-right; references/QR
  in an existing right column; or QR beside the title only when the bottom edge
  will be obstructed. Use a real stable link and test the code.

## Palette and typography

| Direction | primary | accent | Field | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conference Ivory | `#14504A` | `#B2542D` | `#F7F4EC` | Academic and public science |
| Poster Blue | `#1D3F8F` | `#C2410C` | `#F4F6FA` | Engineering, data, systems |
| Night Gallery | `#E0A64A` | `#C96F3E` | `#141414` | Evening exhibitions and screen display |

Night Gallery must cover the whole poster and requires verified contrast. Never
invert a scientific figure unless its source permits it.

Chinese default: `MiSans` title/body + `Microsoft YaHei` labels/references.
Research alternative: `Source Han Serif CN` title/lead + `MiSans` body.
Design/public-facing: `Smiley Sans` title + `MiSans` body.

A2 sizes: title 96–120px; authors 28–34px; affiliation 22–26px; section head
44–54px; body 26–30px; key number 80–120px; caption/source 20–22px; references
18–20px. Multiply by 1.41 for A1 and 2 for A0. Body below 24px at A2 is a defect.

## Evidence and QA

- The main figure is original, complete, and legible at print size. Axes, units,
  n, error bars, and significance marks stay visible and explained.
- Re-plotted data is marked `Redrawn from <source>` and verified against source.
- Key numbers in abstract, results, and conclusion match exactly in unit,
  rounding, n, and preliminary/final status.
- QR points to a stable DOI, repository, or PDF and is tested on a phone. A
  placeholder says `QR placeholder — replace with the real code` and never looks
  scannable.
- Author photos, logos, and institutional marks come from the user or official
  source. No stock imagery, generated evidence, or fake references.
- Do not paste a slide deck onto one page, keep slide footers/transitions, add an
  agenda, use card grids/drop shadows/gradients, or include a second competing
  figure. One poster carries one claim, one main figure, and one takeaway.
