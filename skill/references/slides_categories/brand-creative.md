# Brand & Creative Showcase

**Audience:** brand owner, marketing lead, creative jury, or client decision maker.
**Reader task:** believe the concept, see the system, and approve the direction.
**Default style:** `editorial` in `../design.md`.

## Narrative

```text
cover → opportunity/insight → concept → visual system → campaign/applications
→ proof → closing/ask
```

The deck sells a point of view. Every loud page must be followed by a page that
proves or explains. One concept, one visual rule, one motif.

## Page types

| Page | Job |
|---|---|
| Cover | Subject, proposal type, author/date, and one strong visual anchor. |
| Opportunity | Cultural/business opening, audience insight, brand problem, why now. |
| Concept | One memorable idea sentence, observable pillars, visual rationale. |
| Visual system | Wordmark, palette, type, grid, imagery, motion, and misuse rules. |
| Campaign / applications | The idea across 2–3 real formats and touchpoints. |
| Proof | Audience test, benchmark, pilot, or credible precedent with method/caveat. |
| Closing / ask | Restate concept, one explicit ask, next step/date, contact/credits. |

Optional team/credits, timeline, budget, and production appendix. Keep the pitch
on the idea; production detail goes last.

## Content and evidence contracts

- The cover needs one title, one metadata block, and one image/field, not a
  paragraph explaining the concept.
- Opportunity pages use 2–3 sourced observations and one audience insight. Real
  audience quotes carry role/date; otherwise label the insight as a hypothesis.
- Concept pillars are behaviors, not adjectives. A moodboard is not a decision.
- Visual-system pages show at most six colors and two type families, with at
  least one real application and one misuse. Accessibility claims need contrast
  values.
- Applications show at most three formats and state what stays fixed and what
  can flex across channels.
- Proof uses one object and shows method, population/sample, stimulus, date,
  comparison, and limitation. If no proof exists, say `untested` and propose one.
- Brand assets, product shots, and logos come from the user or official source;
  never redraw or generate them.
- Mockups are labelled as mockups and are not performance proof. Precedents keep
  campaign, year, result source, and ownership clear.
- Generated imagery is expression only, with consent and provenance recorded in
  `assets-prompts.md`. Never generate a person as a testimonial or product as fact.

## Layout recipes

- **Cover / closing:** huge type on one flat field; full-bleed real subject with
  an opaque title block; or concept-justified cropped type with a readable small
  title elsewhere. Closing echoes the cover and makes one explicit ask.
- **Opportunity:** observation left/insight right; a 3–4 signal cultural
  timeline; or category-convention versus unmet-need comparison. Keep it quieter
  than the concept page.
- **Concept:** one 40–64px sentence with three observable pillars in a narrow
  strip, or statement/pillars plus one specimen. A manifesto needs genuinely
  strong copy.
- **Visual system:** palette left/type right with grid and imagery below, or one
  real application with numbered rule callouts. Limit six swatches and two fonts.
- **Campaign / applications:** three aligned formats; one hero application with
  two detail crops; or channel/audience matrix. Label mockups as mockups.
- **Proof:** one test metric with comparison; one before/after pilot with a
  metric; or 2–3 sourced precedents. Show method, sample, date, and caveat.

Alternate loud statement pages with quiet evidence/system pages. Use one main
image per page and no decorative stock filler or collage.

## Palette, typography, and motif

| Direction | primary | accent | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Klein Blue Paper | `#0038B8` | `#2F5FD0` | Modernist systems |
| Ink & Ivory | `#0D0D0D` | `#6D1F2C` | Luxury and editorial |
| Stencil Two-Ink | `#1646B8` | `#FF4F87` | Intentionally loud independent magazine |

For Stencil Two-Ink, body text stays ink black and pink remains under roughly
5% of body pages. Chinese brand default: `Smiley Sans` display + `MiSans` body.
Archival alternative: `Source Han Serif CN` + `Microsoft YaHei` labels.

Cover 72–120px; statement 48–72px; page title 36–48px; section numeral 72–120px;
body 15–18px; caption 10–11px; label/index 9–11px. Choose exactly one motif:
section numeral, corner brackets, overprint mark, or running issue/volume line.

## Anti-homogenization and QA

- Drop or reorder pages when the pitch logic needs it; no fixed deck assembly.
- No two consecutive pages share a composition; never run three full-bleed
  statement pages in a row.
- Use at least four layout grammars in a ten-page deck.
- Do not use generic AI gradients, glassmorphism, rounded card stacks, blobs,
  two motifs, stock photos as evidence, or editorial styling on a business review.
