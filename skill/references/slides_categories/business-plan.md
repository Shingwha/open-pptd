# Business Proposal

**Audience:** customers, partners, investors, and internal sponsors.
**Reader task:** understand the value, believe the plan, and take the next action.
**Default style:** `editorial` in `../design.md`; use `consulting-classic` for a
numbers-heavy investment review.

## Narrative

Choose the shortest narrative that fits the proposal:

```text
problem/opportunity → proposed value → product/solution → evidence/traction →
market and business model → go-to-market or delivery → economics/resources →
risks and ask
```

A sales proposal may emphasize outcomes and implementation; a fundraising deck
may emphasize market, growth engine, and funding use. Do not concatenate every
possible section by reflex.

## Page types

| Page | Job |
|---|---|
| Cover | Specific promise, audience, proposer, date, and one visual anchor. |
| Problem / opportunity | Pain, current alternative, stakes, and why now. |
| Solution / value | What changes for the audience and the measurable promise. |
| Product / delivery | Real workflow, offering, service boundary, or product evidence. |
| Evidence / traction | Customers, pilot, usage, revenue, retention, or validated learning. |
| Market / model | Defined segment, reachable market, pricing, revenue and cost logic. |
| Go-to-market / delivery | Channels, stages, owners, dates, and operational dependencies. |
| Economics / resources | Unit economics, budget, funding, or resources with assumptions. |
| Risks / ask | Main uncertainties, mitigation, decision, amount, and next step. |

Optional competition, roadmap, team, and technical appendix. Team claims and
customer evidence must be real and attributable.

## Content and evidence contracts

- Make the value specific to a named audience; avoid adjectives without proof.
- Show the current alternative and the baseline before claiming an improvement.
- Use real product screens, workflow evidence, pilots, contracts, or customer
  data where available. Mark mockups and proposed features clearly.
- Market size states geography, segment, year, method, and source. Bottom-up
  estimates show their assumptions and range; never invent TAM/SAM/SOM.
- Traction metrics keep period, cohort, denominator, and definition. Revenue,
  bookings, pipeline, and signed intent are not interchangeable.
- Financial projections are labelled projections and show assumptions, horizon,
  unit, and a conservative/base/upside choice when useful.
- Funding/resource asks state amount, use, runway or deliverable, and decision
  date. No fabricated investors, customers, quotes, or traction.
- Risks include trigger, mitigation, owner, and what evidence would change the
  plan. Show the strongest objection fairly.

## Layout recipes

- **Cover:** one bold promise with a real product/subject image, flat field, or
  asymmetric editorial composition. Keep metadata subordinate.
- **Problem:** current workflow left and pain/cost right; three evidence rows;
  or a before/after contrast with one quantified baseline.
- **Solution:** product workflow or 3-stage value chain; real screenshot plus
  three concise outcomes; or one large value statement with supporting proof.
- **Evidence / traction:** one native chart or cohort table plus a reading; a
  pilot before/after; or customer evidence rows with consistent definitions.
- **Market / model:** bottom-up market calculation table; business-model flow;
  pricing/package comparison; or unit-economics bridge using native charts.
- **Go-to-market / delivery:** channel/funnel diagram, phased timeline, or
  owner/action table with absolute dates.
- **Ask:** amount or decision dominates; use-of-funds rows and milestones below;
  closing repeats the cover's visual field and states the next gate.

Alternate expression-led pages with evidence-led pages. Do not use three
consecutive card grids or market-size circles without a calculation.

## Palette and typography

Use the brand or customer reference first. Otherwise choose one direction from
`../design.md` that fits the proposal: editorial for narrative and product work,
consulting-classic for financial scrutiny, dark-tech only with verified contrast.

Use at most two families. Cover 44–64px; section statement 32–48px; page title
28–36px; body 15–19px; annotation 12–14px; caption/source 9–11px; key number
40–64px. One motif may be a stage counter, section tab, corner bracket, or issue
label, never a decorative color bar.

## Anti-homogenization and QA

- Reorder or omit sections to fit the ask. A sales proposal does not need a
  fundraising narrative and vice versa.
- Use at least four layout grammars in a ten-page deck and never repeat a
  composition on consecutive pages.
- Read titles as the value argument and verify every number against its source.
- End with an explicit decision, amount, or next step, not a generic `thank you`.
