# Page recipe · cover

**Purpose:** establish the subject and the creative ambition in one glance.

**Reader task:** feel what kind of brand this is and want to see the idea.

## Slots

1. Title: the proposal's idea or subject (72–120 px).
2. Subject/author: brand, agency/team, date (10–12 px, set apart).
3. One visual anchor: a full-bleed image, a giant numeral, or one saturated
   field. Not a collage.
4. Optional one-line promise or question.

## Variants

**A. Type over field** — a huge title on one flat color field, meta at the
bottom edge. The most reusable.

**B. Full-bleed subject** — a product/place/portrait fills the page; a solid
block carries the title. Keep a text-safe zone.

**C. Cropped type** — display type intentionally cropped by the canvas edge,
with the readable title repeated small in the corner. Only if the concept
justifies it; never lose the title's meaning.

## Selection

A for a strategy-led proposal; B for a product/place-led one; C for a young or
experimental brand. Match the closing page's composition.

## Type and density

Low density. One title, one meta block, one image/field. If the cover needs a
paragraph to explain itself, the concept is not ready.

## Evidence / caption

If the cover uses a real subject photo, credit it in small type or in the
production notes. Generated hero imagery is expression and must be recorded in
`assets-prompts.md`.

## Do / Don't

Do: commit to one typeface and one scale; let the subject be recognizable.
Don't: use a stock "creative team" photo; add three logos; put the agenda on
the cover.
