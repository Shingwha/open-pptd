# Page recipe · closing / ask

**Purpose:** close the loop and make the next step unavoidable.

**Reader task:** know what they are approving and what happens next.

## Slots

1. Closing statement: the concept restated in one line (32–48 px).
2. The ask: approve the direction, fund the pilot, or proceed to production —
   one verb, one scope.
3. Next step and date.
4. Contact/credits.
5. One visual echo of the cover (field, image, or motif).

## Variants

**A. Return to cover** — reuse the cover's composition with the ask in place of
the title. The strongest close.

**B. One line + contact** — a single 40–56 px sentence on a quiet field, with
contact in the corner. Use for a confident, minimal pitch.

**C. Next-step triptych** — three dates/milestones after approval, with the ask
above. Use when the client needs to see the path.

## Selection

A when the cover was strong; B for a design-led jury; C for a production
decision. Never end with "Thank you" alone.

## Type and density

Low density. The ask is the only large object after the statement. Keep the
contact block small and aligned.

## Evidence / caption

No new evidence. Refer back to the proof page if needed. Credits for images and
typefaces go here or in the production notes.

## Do / Don't

Do: ask for one decision; give the date; echo the cover.
Don't: introduce a new idea; list five next steps; end on a stock photo; leave
the ask implicit.
