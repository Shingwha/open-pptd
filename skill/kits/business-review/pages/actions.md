# Page recipe · actions

**Purpose:** convert the review into commitments. This is the page the meeting
should end on.

**Reader task:** know who does what, by when, and how success is measured.

## Slots

1. Title: the action conclusion ("Three moves close the renewal gap by Q4").
2. Action table: action, owner, due date, status, measure of done.
3. Dependency/risk flag for actions that depend on another team.
4. The decision or resource needed from the reader.
5. Source/assumption line if targets are derived.

## Variants

**A. Action table** — 3–6 rows, owner and date visible. The default.

**B. Timeline** — actions on a horizontal time axis with owners above and
milestones below. Use when sequencing matters.

**C. Three commitments** — three large action blocks (not cards): action in
18–22 px, owner/date in small type, one measure each. Use for an executive
audience.

## Selection

A for an operating review; B for a launch or multi-team program; C when the
deck's purpose is to secure three specific commitments.

## Type and density

Medium. Action text 14–16 px; owner/date 11–12 px; status 11 px. Dates are
absolute (`30 Sep 2026`), not relative. No more than six actions.

## Evidence / caption

Targets derive from the trend page; cite it. If an action depends on a
forecast, state the assumption. No owner, no action — leave it out or mark
"owner to be assigned" explicitly.

## Do / Don't

Do: use one verb per action; make the measure observable; flag dependencies.
Don't: use "continue", "monitor", or "improve" as an action; assign multiple
owners; use a Gantt chart with unreadable labels; end with "next steps" and no
date.
